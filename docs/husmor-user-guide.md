# Husmor — Your Meal Planning Assistant

Husmor lives in your Slack channel and helps you plan dinners, build shopping lists, and keep track of what your family likes to eat. Think of her as a warm, experienced housekeeper who knows Norwegian food, cares about nutrition, and hates food waste.

She speaks Norwegian.

---

## Getting started

Just write a message in the Husmor Slack channel. That's it. No commands, no special syntax — just talk to her like you would a person.

Examples:
- "Hei, kan du lage en ukemeny?"
- "Vi har laks i kjoleskapet som ma brukes"
- "Barna vil ha taco pa fredag"

When Husmor is thinking about your message, you'll see a thought bubble (💭) appear on your message. When it disappears, her reply is ready.

---

## What Husmor can do

### Plan weekly dinners

Ask her to suggest a full week of dinners, or just fill in specific days. She considers what's in season, what your family likes, and tries to give you a good mix of fish, meat, and vegetarian meals.

- "Kan du planlegge middag for hele uken?"
- "Vi trenger noe raskt pa tirsdag"
- "Bytt onsdag til noe vegetarisk"

### Build and manage shopping lists

Once you have a plan, ask her to make a shopping list. It gets organized by category (dairy, vegetables, meat, etc.) and synced to a Slack Canvas you can check off while shopping.

You can also add, remove, check off, and clear items dynamically:

- "Lag handleliste for denne uken"
- "Legg til melk og egg pa handlelisten"
- "Fjern brod fra handlelisten"
- "Huk av laks, den har vi kjopt"
- "Slett handlelisten"

Note: "legg pa handlelisten" manages your internal list. If you want to add items to your Oda cart, say "legg i Oda" instead.

### Track what's in your fridge

Tell her what you have at home, and she'll factor it into her suggestions. If something needs to be used soon, she'll suggest meals that use it up.

- "Vi har spinat og feta som ma brukes"
- "Laksen er brukt opp"

### Save recipes

When you find something your family loves, ask Husmor to save the recipe. She'll remember it and suggest it again later.

- "Kan du lagre oppskriften pa laksegryta?"

### Rate meals

After dinner, let Husmor know how it went. She uses this to learn what works and what doesn't.

- "Laksen i dag var kjempegod, 5 av 5!"
- "Barna spiste ikke gronnsaksgryten"

### Track how the kids react

Tell her what the kids thought. Over time, she builds a taste profile for each child and avoids suggesting meals they consistently refuse.

- "Emma elsket tacoen"
- "Oscar ville ikke spise fisken"

### Set family preferences

Allergies, dislikes, household size, cooking skill — just mention it and she'll remember.

- "Vi er fire personer, to voksne og to barn"
- "Ingen notter, Oscar er allergisk"
- "Vi har maks 30 minutter pa hverdager"

### Handle special weeks

Traveling, having guests, or celebrating a holiday? Let her know and she'll adjust the plan.

- "Vi er pa hytta denne uken, trenger enkle retter"
- "Vi far besok av 4 gjester pa lordag"

## Reactions on plans

When Husmor suggests a weekly plan, you can react to it with emoji:

| Emoji | What it does |
|-------|-------------|
| 👍 | Approve the plan |
| 👎 | Reject it |
| 🔁 | Ask her to make a new one |

---

## She learns over time

Husmor gets smarter the more you use her. She picks up on:

- Which meals your family rates highly
- Which days tend to have specific meals (like taco Fridays)
- What categories you keep rejecting
- Feedback you give after meals

Sometimes she'll propose something she thinks she's learned, like "Dere foretrekker raske middager pa tirsdager?" You can confirm with a checkmark or reject with an X.

---

## Proactive check-ins

Husmor occasionally reaches out on her own:

- **Use-it-up reminders** — when items in your fridge need to be used soon
- **Midweek check-in** — asks if the meals so far are working
- **Friday prep** — reminds you about weekend meals and any remaining shopping
- **Sunday summary** — a warm recap of what she learned about your family this week

---

## Tips

- **Be specific** — "Barna liker ikke sopp" is more useful than "vi er kresne"
- **Give feedback** — ratings and comments are the fastest way for her to learn
- **Use threads** — reply in a thread to continue a conversation about a specific topic
- **Just ask** — if you're unsure whether she can do something, just ask. She'll tell you.
